Secure ATM Access — Biometrics & OTP

Done

Nov 2014 – Apr 2015 · BE final-year project

A prototype for secure bank transactions combining biometric authentication with card-reader input. I designed and built the MySQL-backed database with full CRUD functionality, applied object-oriented principles throughout the system design, and focused specifically on reliability: validation logic, structured error handling, and a full pass of user-acceptance testing.

  • MySQL
  • Linux
  • OOP design
  • CRUD
  • UAT

Retail & E-Commerce Assortment Analytics

Done

Jan 2018 – May 2018 · MBA field project, Bangalore

Ran structured customer surveys across small retail outlets and e-commerce sellers in the Bangalore area, cleaned the resulting data for inconsistencies, and built Excel dashboards that surfaced purchasing trends. Used the findings to put together concrete, data-backed recommendations for assortment and purchasing decisions.

  • Excel dashboards
  • Survey design
  • Data cleaning
